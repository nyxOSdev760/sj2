    How CLOAK works

    CLOAK covers the screen with Clever, turns the tab into Clever and mutes your game.

    To get back, do any of these:

    1. 1Press Esc
    2. 2Press your panic key
    3. 3Double-tap (or double-click) the top-left corner of the screen

    On a phone, or after clicking into the Clever page, use the top-left corner.
